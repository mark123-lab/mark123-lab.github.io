What is Augmented Reality?

Augmented Reality (AR) adds digital objects on top of the real world. AR does not replace reality but enhances it with holograms, text, and 3D models.

How Augmented Reality Works

AR works by using cameras and sensors to capture the real-world environment, then processing that data to overlay digital content in a way that aligns with the physical surroundings. This is often achieved through devices like smartphones, tablets, or AR glasses that use GPS, accelerometers, and computer vision to accurately place virtual objects in the user's view.

Applications

  • Mobile AR Games (like Pokémon GO)
  • Virtual Try-On Shopping
  • Smart Navigation
  • Medical Visualization
  • Industrial Training
