Welcome

Discover the world of Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR). Learn how they work, where they are used, and how they shape our future.

The research involved analysis of the virtual reality and the augmented reality in terms of their history and evolution, assigned technology works, advantages, challenges, applications and their relevance and potential impact on biomedical and mechatronics engineering. The research shows that augmented reality (AR) is a sub division of virtual reality (VR). Where virtual uses different equipment like the VR headsets to completely immerse the use into a 3-dimensional simulation while augmented reality is an aspect of virtual reality where 3-D simulations are added to the natural environment to enhance visual aid.
