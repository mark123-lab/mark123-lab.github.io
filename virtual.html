What is Virtual Reality?

Virtual Reality (VR) is a computer-generated 3D environment that fully replaces the real world. It uses VR headsets, controllers, and motion sensors to create immersive digital experiences.

How Virtual Reality Works

It creates this experience by projecting a stereoscpic 3D image and using motion sensors to track the user' head movements in real-time, making the virtual world appear to respond to their actions .This combination tricks the brain into feeling present in the new, digital space with additionl sensory feedback.

Applications

  • Gaming
  • Education & Simulation
  • Medicine & Surgery Training
  • Construction & Design
  • Military Training
